---

*After the results of student evaluations are received*

---

> Father,
>
> As I read the words of my students and their perspectives on the courses I've taught,
>
> And as I look at the numbers and percentiles and the ranks,
>
> May Your mighty hand help me discern and learn, reflect and open my heart for change.
>
> Let the praises not bring pride and assurance in my capabilities, as it all comes from You.
>
> Let the criticisms and disappointments not shadow my heart and draw it into thinking my work was in vain.
>
> I bring it all before you, all these words and numbers, and hold them as an offering, the fruits of my labor,
>
> Teaching and forming, sharing and giving.
>
> May I learn from my mistakes.
> 
> May I find reason to excel in pursuing to serve You better as I review my courses, my techniques, and my *praxis*.
>
> May I humble myself as I seek to serve and love my students with all my heart, all my soul, all my mind,
> 
> As I seek to love You.
>
> Amen.

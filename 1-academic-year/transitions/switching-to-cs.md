*For when discernment lands*

---

> Father,
>
> I come to you today with many feelings,
>
> foremost I thank you for the calling you have placed on my heart and the abounding grace you have given through this opportunity.
>
> I always thank you as I remember the professors who have worked by your benevolent hand in this world.
>
> Because through them you have worked your will upon me, that I may conform to what you call me to in this world, service to my faithful savior, Jesus Christ.
>
> I thank you for the abundant fellowship of the Holy Spirit through this time of chaos, through the moments asking for discernment I thought might never come. I thank you for the discernment brought from those you sent with work in their minds that gave discernment.
>
> I thank you that you are a God of wonder and grace that you may hold me in your hand in the world, that a tear may not fall from my eyes without Your will in Heaven.
>
> I ask always for discernment through times of ease and trouble, for your gift of grace is the only true discernment in this world fallen entirely into sin.
>
> Shape me, Lord, to be your agent on this earth, destroy the foundations of what I have built of my own accord for myself and build a new foundation that does good works for you. I know this may pain me, and I may feel loss, but Lord, your will be done on this earth as it is in heaven.
>
> For when I hear of your great works, and feel your great discernment wash over me I understand this to be your work in my life, Lord.
>
> Thank you Lord for this great opportunity to feel loss, discernment and to be brought closer to you.
>
> I am ever so excited in anticipation for the work that I am to do with this discernment for your Kingdom, oh Holy Lord.
>
> May the grace of God the Father and the abundant fellowship of the Holy Spirit be with me as I transition to this new chapter.
>
> Amen

*For when one of my professors retire*

---

> Through this journey of life Lord,
> 
> I have seen you near and far,
> 
> For everything in this world is Yours.
>
> 
> I know you have plans for all of us,
> 
> And this plan you are enacting will bring joy to many,
> 
> I thank you for the Joy that they have brought in their time here.
>
> 
> Give them strength as you send them from this place,
> 
> Give them strength in their new callings,
> 
> Give them the strength to rest after many years of work for your Glory,
>
> 
> For you are great, and you have given us the command to live by:
> 
> Love your neighbor as ourselves,
> 
> We so have loved having them in our place of study.
>
> 
> And they so lived in love throughout their time here,
> 
> I thank you Father,
> 
> for the oppertunity to get to love and meet them,
> 
> To be mentored by them,
> 
> To laugh with them,
> 
> To have a chance to talk seriously with them about what matters to us,
>
> 
> We thank you for the oppertunities you have given them,
> 
> That they may show how amazing our your deeds, Lord,
> 
> And how we may delight in studying them.
>
> 
> We may have peace in knowing that,
> 
> Not a hair can fall from our heads,
> 
> Not a professor can retire,
> 
> Not a day can be had,
> 
> Without your will in heaven, Lord.
>
> 
> We pray in your great and holy name, Lord.
> 
> Amen

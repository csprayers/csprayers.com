*With great gratitude*

---

> Thank you Graceful Lord,
>
> For you sent your only son, Jesus Christ, to die for our sins.
>
> For with this sacrifice we have a chance to have a mediator with you.
>
> In the same way I thank you for moving my professor with your Holy Spirit, that you also sent, to provide care for me. I thank you for allowing them to do a good work and follow your will.
>
> I ask to give them Grace and peace from you Father, and from the Lord Jesus Christ.
>
> For I always thank you, God, as I remember my Professor in my prayers because I see how their faith in the Lord Jesus has shaped them.
>
> I pray that they may be active in sharing of their faith, so that they will have a full understanding of every good thing we have in Christ.
>
> Your love through your work in my professor through the spirit has given me great discernment, joy, and all the fruits of the Spirit. For these things can only come from you, and because of this have refreshed my joy in faith of you, Great and Holy Lord.
>
> Father in heaven, may your will be done on this earth that your Glory be shown as it is shown endlessly in heaven.
>
> I ask for the peace of God the Father, and the abundant fellowship of the Holy Spirit be with my professor through all their days, that they may serve you, and you alone on this earth.
>
> Let them continue to show these fruits of the spirit as You transform their lives continuously through your Great Grace and Mercy, Lord of Heaven and Earth.
>
> Amen

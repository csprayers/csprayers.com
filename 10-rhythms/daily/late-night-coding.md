*Working into darkness*

---

> Yahweh Great and Infinite Creator God

> While the sleep and my exhaustion catch up to me, you are here
> Through every long night, and character typed you are with me
>
> I praise your Great and Holy name, Lord,
> For you are worthy of it all
>
> Let your will be done, and all of this task be done in your Glory
> as I endure through the temptation of rest

> I praise you for the blessing of the strength you have given me
> For the oppertunity to endure strenuous times
>
> For times as strenuous have come before,
> Bring those difficulties to mind,
> For my endurance was done only by Your Great Hand,
> And your Watch over me, that endurance was possible

> I Praise that you have given, in your word what is Good,
> 
> That you turned formless to form, and form to order
> I am also turning the formless code into order,
> For your Glory that I work, and code

> Bring to my eyes sight to see the flaws in my work tonight,
> so that it may bring praise to you evermore
>
> That I know in my weakness I will stumble without you,
> so may this time be with you, showing the Glory of your work on this earth

> I thank you Lord for the gift of your hope, for no matter what challenges approach
> That my Father is waiting for me in Heaven,
> That I may do work for the Glory of God,
> Even though I am so wholly corrupted by sin.

> Lord, open my ears to knowledge, that I may not be blinded by what I think I know,
> For you are the Truth, The Word, and The Only.

> Remind me that you are soverign in every bump along the way and in every great success,
> For all of these are done for your Glory.

> May the code I work on reflect your light,
> For your Son has brought the upmost joy and comfort to this world of Sin.
>
> This light I work by reminds me of the light that you placed within me,
> Although this light may go out when I rest, your light will never falter,
> For you never cast a shifting shadow, and you are Infinite, Forever.
>
> May this light show on my neighbors with
> hospitable code,
> late night advice,
> errors that teach.
>
> For I know at the end of this night, when this lamp may dim to nothing,
> It is time to rest, as you have shown,
> Remind me to rest, as this nights endurance will reflect in the next morning,
> But, this earthly endurance is naught, for the real endurance is your Infinite Wisdom.


> May I not get distracted from the things that will not bring you glory, Lord.
>
> For the world is tempting me, with a short click to instant earthly pleasure,
>
> I know you are within me, and have given me the endurance for this task,
>
> So I ask for this continued, for my only want is to praise your name and speak of your Glory forever, Lord.

> Lord, be with me tonight as the light that endures,
> The light that is Infinite,
> The light that drew me to You,
> The light that calls me closer to you,
>
> For my endurance is from you, and you alone.
>
> Amen

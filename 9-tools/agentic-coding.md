*Before working with AI coding agents*

---

> Heavenly Father,
>
> whose words created all things from nothing,
>
> and who has redeemed us through the Word of Christ:

> Thank you for giving us technology with which we can create using our words.
>
> Use it to point our minds and hearts to Christ,
>
> who created and sustains all things through the word of your power.
>
> Remind us how contingent our words are:
>
> before a word is on our lips, you know it completely.
>
> And we can form no new thing under the Sun,
>
> only what you have already known.

> I am about to use a technology that promises to deliver
>
> on any desire that I can express in words.
>
> Shape my desires rightly
>
> so that they would lead to life and righteousness.
>
> May your Kingdom come, and your will be done.

> Lead me not into temptation as I work with this technology.
>
> These tools make it easy for me to accept without understanding,
>
> tempting me to shirk my responsibility for the behavior of what I make.
>
> The promise of unlimited productivity tempts me to avoid rest;
>
> help me to know when to stop.
>
> The intermittent rewards of checking in on the progress of agentic tasks
>
> risks gambling-like addiction;
>
> deliver me from this evil.

> Draw my awareness towards your presence with me—
>
> before, during, and after my work.

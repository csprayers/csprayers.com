*Out of equilibrium*

---

> Glorious God
> 
> Perfect Lord
>
> Creator of all that is Good

> We ask for self control so that we do not let our walls be broken down,
> broken down by the busyness in our lives,
> broken down by our temptations to do wrong,
> broken down by choosing not to do what is right even when we know what is right,
> broken down by letting the world taking control of our lives.
>
> For we know in life and death,
> We are yours forever.
>
> Yours in Eternal Love,
> And by your Son, Jesus Christ,
> Our Lord and Saviour.
>
> Let us be reminded of the sacrifice of our Holy Lord, Jesus Christ,
> So we may sacrifice the parts of our habits that do not Glorify you, Lord.
>
> Let us sacrifice
> 
> Our Procrastination,
> Choosing to not do what we ought to do,
> even when we know what is right for your Glory,
>
> Our Love for Human Satisfaction,
> Choosing to befriend the world,
> Making us an enemy of you, God.
>
> Our selfish ambition,
> Wanting to become the lords of our own lives.
>
> For we know that you, Lord, are our Father in Heaven,
> Our Protector, Who sent His Son, Christ Jesus, to die for our sin.
>
> Let us Glorify you with our work, all of it that is Good, and for your Glory.
>
> Let us Delight in sacrificing the busyness of this world, for you, God. 
>
> Keep our eyes fixed on your Wisdom, that above all knowledge in this world,
> Your Wisdom that endures forever, and grants us Eternal Life.
>
> For we must remember the King of Kings who we work for on this earth,
> The Lord of our Lives, and the Protector of us.
> We will not work for the obscure men of this world,
> But for you, Lord.
>
> We ask for your Divine Guidance and Wisdom to guide us in the tasks ahead,
> Which tasks we need to sacrifice, so that we may Glorify you in all that we do.
>
> We ask for Rest, from the constant motion of this world,
> For you have shown us what is right, and we alone cannot uphold all things,
> We put our faith in you Alone, Christ, God, and Holy Spirit.
>
> Let the Holy Spirit guide us to doing what is right for you in our every action,
> For our lives belong completely to you, Lord.
>
> Amen.
